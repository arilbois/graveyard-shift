# Wellness safety, informed consent and privacy

- This product encourages awareness and breaks; it does not monitor health or offer medical diagnosis.
- Never infer dehydration, poor posture, burnout, depression, eye damage, or fatigue from IDE events.
- User-confirmed human check-ins are optional and may remain only in session memory unless explicitly enabled for local saving.
- Track anonymous aggregate durations, reminder outcomes and user-initiated breaks, not document contents, keystrokes, paths, secrets, screenshots or microphones/camera.
- `idleMinutes=5` means activity estimates stop after lack of IDE signals; idle is not automatically a healthy break. Do not credit a break without user confirmation.
- Distinguish quick eye rest from meaningful extended breaks. A 20-second eye rest must not reset a five-hour long-session warning.
- Five hours is a configurable product trigger, not a medically validated safety threshold. Avoid wording implying specific disease risk from exactly five hours.
- Reminder escalation must be calm: never prevent closing, never block saves or tasks, never use loud sounds without control or repeated intrusive messages.
- Long-session panel is default. Any voluntary immersive/fullscreen presentation requires affirmative opt-in; do not present unsupported desktop overlays as feasible.
- Snooze and dismiss are valid without guilt messaging; cap frequency and add a cooldown to avoid harassment.
- Reduced motion and accessibility settings override theatrical design for that user.
- Ensure offline operation and provide a local data clear/reset action.
